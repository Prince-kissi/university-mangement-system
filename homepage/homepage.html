Welcome to

UMAT Complaint Management System

A transparent, efficient and user-friendly platform for students to submit complaints and get them resolved. The system allows students to submit, receive, and respond to complaints at any time and from any location, reducing the need for paperwork. Complaints are automatically routed to the appropriate department helping to speed up investigations, responses, and resolutions. The system promotes openness by allowing secure and confidential reporting without fear of retaliation.
