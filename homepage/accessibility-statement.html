UMAT aims to provide a complaint platform that can be used by students with different devices, abilities, network conditions, and assistive technologies.

Our Commitment

The platform is designed to support readable text, visible focus states, meaningful labels, keyboard navigation, responsive layouts, and clear error messages. Accessibility is treated as an ongoing practice, not a one-time launch task.

Accessibility Features

Responsive layoutsPages adjust for phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop screens.
Readable structureHeadings, labels, and form sections are organized for scanning.
Keyboard supportInteractive controls are built to remain reachable by keyboard.

Known Limits and Support

Some uploaded evidence files, third-party embeds, older browsers, or externally produced documents may not meet the same accessibility quality as the core website. If you need an alternative way to submit a complaint or receive a document, contact the appropriate office for assistance.

Accessibility Feedback

When reporting an accessibility issue, include the page URL or page name, the device and browser you used, the assistive technology involved if any, and a short description of what happened.

Contact: registrar@umat.edu.gh or the Registrar's Office, UMAT Administration Block, Tarkwa.
