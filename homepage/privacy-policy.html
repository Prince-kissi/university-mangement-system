UMAT uses the Complaint Management System to receive, route, investigate, and respond to student complaints. The information you provide is handled with care and shared only with people who need it to resolve your case.

Overview

This policy explains what information may be collected when you use the platform, why it is needed, and how it is protected. It applies to complaint submission, complaint tracking, account registration, support requests, and anonymous reporting features.

Purpose limited Information is collected to manage complaints, support users, and protect platform integrity.
Access controlled Only authorized staff and assigned departments can access case information.
Transparent updates Students can track complaint status and receive updates through official channels.

Information We Collect

The platform may collect information you enter directly, information needed to verify your account, and technical information needed to keep the service secure.

CategoryExamplesReason
Identity detailsName, index number, reference number, student email.To verify access and connect complaints to the correct account.
Complaint detailsCategory, department, incident date, description, evidence files.To route, investigate, and resolve the complaint.
Contact detailsEmail, phone number, preferred response method.To send updates or request clarification.
Technical detailsDevice information, browser type, security logs.To maintain security, reliability, and audit trails.

How We Use Information

  • Register and authenticate student accounts.
  • Create complaint records and assign them to relevant departments.
  • Communicate status updates, responses, and resolution notes.
  • Investigate misuse, duplicate submissions, security threats, or abuse of anonymous reporting.
  • Improve platform performance, accessibility, and support services.

Information Sharing

Complaint information is shared only with authorized offices, assigned departments, investigators, and platform administrators who need access for official complaint handling. UMAT does not sell student complaint data.

Anonymous complaints: If you choose anonymous reporting, the platform hides your identifying student details from the receiving department where the workflow supports that protection. Some technical records may still be retained for safety, rate limiting, and audit purposes.

Retention and Security

Complaint records are retained for as long as required for case handling, institutional records, audit, dispute resolution, and service improvement. Security controls may include restricted access, password protection, encrypted connections, administrative logging, and periodic review of access rights.

Your Choices and Requests

You may request correction of inaccurate account details, ask about how your complaint information is handled, or request help accessing your records. Some information may need to be retained when required for official investigations, safety, audit, or institutional record keeping.
