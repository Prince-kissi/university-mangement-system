Our Story

Built for students, by people who listen

The University of Mines and Technology Complaint Management System was created to give every student a clear, dignified channel to raise concerns — whether about academics, finances, facilities, or wellbeing.

Before this platform, complaints were often scattered across emails, hand-written notes, and word-of-mouth requests that could get lost along the way. We built a single, secure portal so nothing falls through the cracks and every voice is heard.

Today, the system routes complaints automatically to the right department, keeps students updated in real time, and protects the privacy of every individual who comes forward.

Purpose

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide UMAT students with a transparent, efficient, and confidential platform for raising complaints — ensuring every concern is acknowledged, investigated, and resolved promptly by the appropriate department.

Our Vision

To become a model institution-wide standard for student grievance handling in Ghana — where accountability, openness, and care for students are built into every interaction.
