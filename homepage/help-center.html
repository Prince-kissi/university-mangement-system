Quick Answers

What should I include in a complaint?

Include the category, department, incident date, location, a clear description, and any evidence that helps the assigned office investigate.

How do I know my complaint was received?

The system provides a reference number after submission. Keep it safe so you can track progress later.

Can I submit without revealing my name?

Yes. Use the anonymous complaint page or the anonymous option on the complaint form when available.

Who can see my complaint?

Access is limited to authorized staff, assigned departments, and administrators involved in handling the complaint.
