Tips for a Strong Complaint
  • 1Be specific — include dates, places, and names of people involved.
  • 2Attach any supporting documents, screenshots, or evidence.
  • 3Choose the correct department so your complaint reaches the right team faster.
  • 4Keep your reference number safe — you'll need it to track progress.
  • 5Avoid vague language; describe exactly what happened and what outcome you expect.
