Before You Submit

  1. Remove personal details from the description if you do not want the receiving department to infer your identity.
  2. Keep dates, locations, and evidence specific enough for investigation.
  3. Use a safe contact method only if you are comfortable receiving follow-up.
  4. Call campus security or the appropriate emergency contact for immediate safety threats.
