On this page What Cookies Are Types We Use Managing Cookies Updates
Short version The portal should use only the storage needed for login, security, preferences, accessibility, and service diagnostics.

Cookies are small data files stored by your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, can remember choices or keep a session active while you use the portal.

What Cookies Are

Cookies help websites remember information about your visit. For a complaint platform, they may support secure login, form progress, anti-spam protections, accessibility preferences, and maintenance diagnostics.

Types We May Use

TypePurposeCan it be disabled?
EssentialAuthentication, security, form session handling, and abuse prevention.Some features may stop working if disabled.
PreferenceRemembering display settings, accessibility choices, and saved form preferences.Yes, but settings may reset.
PerformanceUnderstanding reliability, page errors, and load speed in aggregate.Yes, if the production system provides a preference control.
Third-partyEmbedded tools such as maps or support widgets may set their own cookies.Manage through browser or third-party controls.

Managing Cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, you may not be able to log in, submit a complaint, upload evidence, or track complaint progress properly.

Browser settingsUse browser privacy settings to clear or block cookies.
Security impactDisabling essential cookies can interrupt secure sessions.
Preference reviewProduction deployments may add a cookie preference panel.

Updates to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the platform adds new integrations, analytics, security controls, or preference tools. The updated date at the top of this page will be changed when revisions are made.
