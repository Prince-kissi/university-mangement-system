By accessing or using the UMAT Complaint Management System, you agree to use the platform for legitimate institutional complaint handling and related support activities.

Acceptance of Terms

These Terms and Conditions govern use of the complaint portal, including account registration, complaint submission, complaint tracking, support requests, and anonymous reporting. If you do not agree with these terms, do not use the platform.

Account Access and Responsibilities

  • You must provide accurate student or contact information when required.
  • You are responsible for keeping your password and reference details secure.
  • You must notify the appropriate office if you suspect unauthorized account access.
  • UMAT may restrict access where misuse, security risk, or false information is suspected.

Complaint Submissions

Complaints should be honest, relevant, and submitted in good faith. You are encouraged to provide enough detail for the assigned department to understand the issue, including dates, locations, supporting documents, and preferred contact method where appropriate.

Anonymous reporting: Anonymous complaints are accepted where supported by the workflow, but misuse, spam, harassment, or knowingly false submissions may be limited or investigated.

Acceptable Use

You agree not to use the system to upload harmful files, impersonate another person, submit abusive content, interfere with the platform, attempt unauthorized access, or make threats. UMAT may remove content or suspend access where needed to protect users and the institution.

Service Availability

The platform is intended to remain available for complaint submission and tracking, but scheduled maintenance, network issues, security updates, or emergency outages may affect access. For urgent matters, contact the relevant university office directly.

Changes to These Terms

UMAT may update these terms to reflect operational, security, legal, or service changes. Continued use of the platform after updates means you accept the revised terms.
